# Juzgo FAQ — data plans, top-ups & eSIM validity

Draft copy for the Juzgo FAQ page. Written for travelers with no technical background.
Numbers marked with * are Airalo defaults and should be confirmed per-package once the

## What does "Unlimited" data actually mean?

"Unlimited" means you won't be cut off or hit a hard data wall during your plan's validity
period — but it's not unlimited at full speed all day, every day.

Here's how it actually works: you get a set amount of high-speed data each day (typically
3 GB\*). Once you use that, your speed drops to a slower rate (around 1 Mbps\*) for the
rest of that 24-hour period. At that reduced speed you can still message, browse, use
maps, and make basic video calls — but streaming video or large downloads will feel slow.
Your full speed comes back automatically at the next daily reset, which is 24 hours after
your eSIM first activated (not midnight).

This cycle repeats every day for the length of your plan. You're never fully disconnected
— just slower on days you use a lot of data.

The exact daily high-speed amount can vary by destination — check the specific details on
your plan's page before you buy.

## Do I need to buy a new eSIM every time I visit the same country?

Not necessarily. If your original plan supports top-ups, you can add more data to the
same eSIM instead of installing a new one — useful if you're a repeat visitor or extending
your stay.

Not every plan supports this. Whether a plan is "rechargeable" is shown on its product
page before you buy. If a plan isn't rechargeable, or your original eSIM has expired,
you'll need to buy a fresh eSIM — this only takes a couple of minutes.

## How do I know if I can top up my old eSIM, or if I need a new one?

Go to **My Purchases** and tap **Check** next to the plan in question. Juzgo checks your
eSIM's live status and tells you one of three things:

- **Top up available** — your eSIM is still active and can be recharged with more data
- **Buy a new eSIM** — your plan doesn't support top-ups, or the top-up window has closed
- **Expired** — your eSIM's validity has ended and it can no longer be used

We always check this live rather than guessing from your purchase date, since validity
and top-up windows vary by plan and destination.

## My data ran out before my trip ended — can I still use my eSIM?

If your plan is rechargeable, yes — top it up from **My Purchases** and you'll be back
online right away, no reinstall needed.

If it's not rechargeable, you'll need a new eSIM. Your original eSIM's remaining validity
days (if any) can't be transferred or refunded.

## What countries does my regional or global plan actually cover?

Every regional (e.g. Asia, Europe) or global bundle shows a **View list of countries**
link on its product page — tap it to see the full list of countries and networks included.
Some global bundles cover 100+ countries, so this list is searchable.

## Can I find a country plan even when I don't know if it's sold as its own plan or only inside a bundle?

Yes — search for any country name and Juzgo shows every option that covers it: a
country-specific plan if one exists, plus any regional or global bundle that includes it.
Results are grouped from narrowest (just that country) to broadest (global), so you can
compare price against coverage.
